# Director, Manufacturing Engineering

Northfield Surgical is scaling production of its powered surgical stapler.
The Director of Manufacturing Engineering will lead manufacturing engineering
and test engineering through the transfer of the next-generation stapler from
development to high-volume production.

## Responsibilities

- Lead a team of 15 manufacturing, process, and test engineers.
- Own design transfer and process validation (IQ/OQ/PQ) for new production lines.
- Drive yield improvement and scrap reduction using SPC and DOE.
- Partner with supplier quality on supplier qualification and single-source risk.
- Prepare manufacturing for FDA and notified body audits.

## Requirements

- 12+ years in medical device manufacturing engineering, 5+ in people leadership.
- Experience with Class II or Class III devices under ISO 13485 and 21 CFR 820.
- Hands-on process validation and design transfer experience.
- Experience with electromechanical assemblies and automated test.
- Bachelor's degree in engineering; advanced degree preferred.
- Lean or Six Sigma certification preferred.
